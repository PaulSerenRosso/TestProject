# How chemistry is learned and practiced

Date: 2026-10-15

## Summary
Chemistry is taught concepts-first in high school, with short guided practicals. University adds longer protocol-based labs and instruments. Professional chemists learn by supervised work and written procedures (SOPs). Simulations are widely used for concepts; full virtual labs mostly at university for pre-lab preparation.

## Key points
| Level | Learning | Lab practice | Software | Type |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| High school | Atoms, reactions, acids/bases, concentrations | Short guided practicals in groups (TP in France): dilutions, titrations, simple reactions, pH. Teachers and lab technicians prepare benches | Simulations (PhET), sensors, spreadsheets | Practice (varies by country) |
| University | General, organic, physical, analytical | Longer labs with protocols, safety training, lab notebooks; first-years do dilutions and titrations; later multi-step synthesis and instruments | Structure drawing (ChemDraw), computational chemistry, some virtual labs | Practice |
| Professional | On the job, from supervisors and SOPs | Reproducible, documented, quality-controlled work | LIMS, instrument software, process simulators | Practice |

- **Fact (verified):** French inspectorate report says hands-on experimental work in physics-chemistry falls short of programme recommendations, partly for lack of reduced-size groups.
- **Fact (verified):** Lab technicians apply and enforce hygiene and safety rules and prepare practicals.
- **Fact (verified):** PhET reported 250M+ simulation runs and 50M+ learners in 2025 (all subjects; no chemistry-only figure found).
- **Fact (verified):** Virtual and hands-on labs give similar conceptual scores; hands-on is better for physical setup skills (RSC 2013, electrochemistry). A 2026 RSC study found virtual labs worked better for high prior-knowledge students and may widen gaps.
- **Fact (verified):** ACS prefers hands-on labs for chemistry majors (as cited in the 2013 paper).
- **Fact (verified):** VR barriers are cost, teacher training, lack of long-term studies (DCU review, 31 studies). Students found a Quest 2 virtual lab awkward because manual skills differ from a real lab (Maribor 2024, 6 students).
- **Fact (verified):** Lab accident causes include poor dilution technique, rushed work and inadequate PPE. US CSB counted 261 lab incidents 2001-2018, 130 at universities.
- **Judgment:** Usage of Labster and ChemCollective at university for pre-lab preparation is from background knowledge, not verified here.

## Use cases for the app
- Pre-lab rehearsal of procedures and safe failure of a hazardous step (first-year university, vocational, older high school).
- Teacher-parametrized variants of a standard experiment (for example a different unknown concentration per student).
